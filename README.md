# Around

**Everything local. One place.**

Around is a community-focused web platform designed to bring local residents, businesses, services, and commerce together in one easy-to-use place.

Around is currently in early development and is being built by **Milnova Software Solutions**.

## About Around

Around is designed to serve as a digital community hub where residents can discover what is happening around them without needing to navigate multiple websites, social media groups, or marketplaces.

The initial version is being developed for Lyons, Kansas, with the platform designed to support additional communities in the future.

## Planned Features

### Community
Create posts, share announcements, ask questions, and participate in local conversations.

### Marketplace
List items for sale and browse items being sold by people in the local community.

### Auctions
Create timed auctions, place bids, and view active local auctions.

### Services
Find local service providers such as handymen, lawn care providers, plumbers, cleaners, mechanics, and other independent professionals.

### Local Deals
Allow local restaurants and businesses to share specials, promotions, and other local offers.

## Technology

Around is initially being developed using:

- Python
- Streamlit
- GitHub

Additional technologies will be introduced as the platform develops.

## Status

Around is currently in early development and is not yet intended for public use.
